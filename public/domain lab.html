Verdict Logic

What the scanner is actually proving.

Live: a registered domain has positive web evidence (root/www HTTP probe or a directly loaded favicon), with DNS support.
Available: RDAP says the domain is not registered and DNS resolvers do not contradict it. Confirm with a registrar before purchase.
Parked: strong parking / marketplace nameserver evidence was detected. Generic registrar DNS alone is not treated as parking.
Registered: the domain exists, but this scan did not prove an active website.
